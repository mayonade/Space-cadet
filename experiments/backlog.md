# Test backlog

Ranked by expected effect on booked calls. The analyst re-ranks after every readout.
Nothing here has been run; expected effects are hypotheses, not findings.

| # | Test | Channel | Why it could matter | Needs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Outreach vs global holdout | All | Tells us whether the program adds calls at all, and the true cost per call | Running by default |
| 2 | Angle: "find the right fit" vs "a second act after corporate" vs "how people actually pay for it" | Email | The angle is usually the biggest lever; winner feeds every channel | Email sender |
| 3 | First email asks a one-line question vs links to the booking page | Email | Replies start conversations; a hard ask may burn cold leads | Email sender |
| 4 | Email only vs email + handwritten note on day 5 | Mail, Tier A | Is mail worth its cost on the best leads | Mail vendor |
| 5 | Email only vs email + retargeting ads | Ads | Measures ads by audience holdout, not platform-claimed conversions | Ad account, pixel |
| 6 | Lead magnet: fit quiz vs buyer's guide PDF | Email, landing page | A quiz collects answers that improve scoring | Landing page |
| 7 | Email only vs email + LinkedIn connect on day 1 | LinkedIn, Tier A/B | Whether a familiar face lifts reply rate | LinkedIn URLs on leads |
| 8 | Trigger mail: postcard to booking-page abandoners vs email reminder only | Mail | Highest-intent moment in the funnel | Pixel, mail vendor |
| 9 | Call within 1 hour of a click vs next business day | Phone | Speed to lead is often decisive | Click alerts |
| 10 | Old leads (12+ months): "still looking?" one-liner vs standard sequence | Email | Most of the list is old; a cheap way to find the live ones | Email sender |
| 11 | Handwritten note vs printed postcard | Mail, Tier A | Note costs several times more; does it earn it | Mail vendor |
| 12 | Information kit before the intro call vs none | Mail | May raise show rate and move candidates further | Fulfillment |
| 13 | Sender: consultant's name vs firm name | Email | Personal senders usually win; verify | Email sender |
| 14 | Sequence length: 5 emails in 21 days vs 8 in 30 | Email | More touches vs more unsubscribes | Email sender |
